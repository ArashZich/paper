Paper makes your whole phone look like an e-reader (Kindle, Boox), inside every app and not only on the home screen.

• No colors, paper-grey instead of glaring white, bold black text, no animations
• One On/Off button, one slider for the paper tone, an optional warm-light slider for night reading
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile
• Tiny (about 40 KB), no background service, no internet access, no ads or tracking
• Turning it off puts every system setting back exactly as it was

Paper uses Android's own display settings. Android requires a one-time permission that can only be granted over adb:

adb shell pm grant ir.arashzich.papermode android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS

The app shows this command with a copy button.
