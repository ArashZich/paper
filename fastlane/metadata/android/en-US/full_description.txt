Paper makes your whole phone look like an e-reader, inside every app and not only on the home screen.

• Grayscale (e-ink colors), blue-light filter, extra dim, high-contrast text, no animations
• Separate Day and Night looks, each fully adjustable
• Modes: Off, Day, Night, Auto (switches on a schedule you choose)
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile
• Tiny (about 45 KB), no background service, no internet access, no ads or tracking
• Turning it off puts every system setting back exactly as it was

Paper uses Android's own display settings (color correction, Night Light, Extra dim). Android requires a one-time permission that can only be granted over adb:

adb shell pm grant ir.arashzich.papermode android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS

The app shows this command with a copy button.
